# Device research notes

Notes from reading other open projects and Corsair's documentation on
2026-11-15, for `docs/DEVICES_PLAN.md`. They record protocol facts, with
sources, and mark each as [V] verified in source or [I] inferred. No code
or data tables were copied: the sources are GPL and Nexus Open is MIT (see
the plan's clean-room rule). Facts here are unconfirmed on our hardware
until a phase tests them.

- `openlinkhub.md` — OpenLinkHub (GPL-3.0-or-later): the Commander Core,
  LCD cap, K100 and Nexus at byte level, and its protocol families.
- `others.md` — liquidctl, OpenRGB, ckb-next, kernel hwmon drivers, Rust
  crates.
- `scope.md` — iCUE's features, platform permissions, risks, and what
  users want from an alternative.
